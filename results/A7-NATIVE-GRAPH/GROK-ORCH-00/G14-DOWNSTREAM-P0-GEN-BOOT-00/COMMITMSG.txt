G14 P0: reject dirty DDR P_BOOT header as GEN=FFFFFFFF

P_BOOT used to accept any DDR word with bit0=1 and gen!=0.
All-ones DRAM therefore became live_gen=0xFFFFFFFF, vis_w stamped 0xFF,
and C7 ack_count advanced without architectural commits.

XSim TWO_FREE (30 vis_w + 2 free) reproduces silicon HOLD_A pack
2322838281802120. Header must now match FLUSH format
{31'd0, gen, 1'b1} with gen<=WRAP_LIMIT, else P_CLR.
