## Findings raised during the fix work, and why they matter

**The contrast checker was wrong in the same direction as the comments it
replaced.** It classified every semantic colour as a graphical object at 3:1.
They are all pill and badge *text* at 10–12px. axe running against real
rendered stories caught `--gb-inactive` at 4.24:1. A checker that encodes the
author's intent rather than the rendered reality passes while the product
fails. Fixed by reclassifying, and by moving the two text usages of
`--gb-inactive` to `--gb-text-muted` rather than brightening a token whose job
is to look dim.

**`TabNav` and `ProcessStrip` reached into the module registry.** They could
not be rendered in isolation, which is why the first attempt at their stories
failed. Both now receive `tabs` as a prop and `AppShell` is the single place
that reads `implementedTabs()`. This is the
`state-decouple-implementation` rule from `vercel-composition-patterns`, and
the story requirement is what surfaced the violation.

**A dependency defect blocked all story tests.** `@testing-library/dom` pinned
`aria-query` 5.3.0 alongside the root 5.3.2, and the addon's setup file failed
to import `elementRoles`. Deduping via an `overrides` entry was necessary but
not sufficient: `aria-query` is CommonJS, so `optimizeDeps.include` was also
required for Vite to expose its named exports. Both fixes carry a comment
explaining why, because a future dependency bump will make them look removable.
